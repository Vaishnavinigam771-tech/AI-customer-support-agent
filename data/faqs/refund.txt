SHOPEASE REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and returned in their original condition.

Damaged or defective products may qualify for a refund or replacement.

Refunds are generally processed within 5-7 business days after the returned product is inspected.

Shipping charges are normally non-refundable unless the product was defective or the wrong product was delivered.

Digital products are not eligible for refunds after download.
